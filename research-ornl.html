Research focus

The LIBS lab at Oak Ridge National Laboratory uses laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy (LIBS) to characterize samples for advanced reactor purposes. This includes characterizing the off-gas seen in molten salt reactors, which is relevant as MSRs can release aersolized and gaseous radioactive byproducts.

My responsibilities

I designed an factorial designed experiment using double pulse-LIBS to find signal-to-noise ratios of argon gas under different configurations. This included different laser energies, interpulse delays, gate delays, and gas flow rates.
