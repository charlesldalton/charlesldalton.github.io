Research focus

The research at Nuclear Security and Sensing Laboratory uses optical diagnostic techniques, such as laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy (LIBS), for deterrence and forensic purposes. I work with Justin Borrero on analyzing plume formation and plasma morphology for various materials.

My responsibilities

I conduct the optical diagnostics, aligning and operating the imaging and spectroscopy setups, running ablation experiments, and processing the resulting image and spectral data. This includes writing scripts to track characteristics of induced plasmas. Much of my contribution has been in analysis methods.
