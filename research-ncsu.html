Research focus

Molten salts are a leading candidate coolant and fuel carrier for advanced reactors, and their optical properties carry chemical information, including oxidation states, corrosion products, dissolved species, that is otherwise hard to obtain without removing and quenching a sample. The MUSCL lab at NCSU designs new methods for performing spectroscopic analysis on molten salts.

My responsibilities

Alongside working with graduate students in their own research endeavors, I designed and built an optical furnace for performing spectroscopy on molten salt samples. I then configured the optical path and collected UV-Vis spectra on molten eutectics. I presented my research finding at the NEUP annual review meeting in Raleigh in July 2025.
