About

Research interests

  • Off-gas monitoring for molten salt reactorsReal-time optical measurement of noble fission gases in a flowing stream, where sampling is slow and grab samples are a safeguards liability.
  • Laser diagnostics in extreme environmentsSingle- and double-pulse LIBS, emission imaging, and absorption spectroscopy applied to melts, plumes, and gases that cannot be instrumented conventionally.
  • Safeguards and material accountancyInstruments that produce a continuous, tamper-evident record of what is in a fuel cycle rather than a periodic snapshot.
  • Statistics of noisy measurementsFactorial design, robust inference, and profile fitting that hold up when the signal is faint and the shot-to-shot variance is the physics.
  • Bench to deploymentThe engineering distance between a technique that works on an optical table and one that survives in a reactor building.
